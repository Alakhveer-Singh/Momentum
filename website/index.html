FAQ

Good questions.

Is it really free?

Yes. Momentum is free for small businesses, and you don't need a credit card to sign up.

Is my data private?

Your customer list belongs to you. No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold. You can export everything to CSV any time.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen like an app.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Invite your team as admins, managers or reps. Reps only see the customers and follow-ups assigned to them.

Can I import from Excel?

Yes. Save your sheet as a CSV file and import it. All your customers come in at once.
